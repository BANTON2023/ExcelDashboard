## Vocabulary

- **A-roll:** the main footage (here, the two people talking).
- **B-roll:** extra footage or images cut in over the A-roll to show what is being
  talked about.
- **Rough cut:** removing pauses, mistakes and dead air.
- **Jump cut:** a cut that joins two parts of the same shot, so the picture "jumps".
- **Punch-in:** a quick zoom on the speaker for emphasis or to hide a jump cut.
- **Export / render:** turning the editable project into a finished MP4 file.
